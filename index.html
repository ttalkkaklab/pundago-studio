Pundago Studio

The desktop workflow behind Pundago’s short films. It connects the channel’s TikTok account to Social Flow and sends approved videos to the TikTok inbox as drafts.

Visit @pundagolab on TikTok

How the connection works

  1. Start the TikTok connection from Social Flow and sign in on TikTok’s authorization page.
  2. Review the requested permissions. The desktop app checks the authorized account’s username before it stores the connection.
  3. Select and approve a video to send a draft. Open TikTok to review it, edit it, and decide whether to publish.

The integration is in testing. Read the Social Flow setup documentation.
