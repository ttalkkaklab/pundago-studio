Terms of service

These terms apply to the Pundago Studio documentation site and its TikTok integration.

Account and content

Connect only a TikTok account you can manage. Upload only videos you have permission to use. Follow TikTok’s terms and community rules.

Draft workflow

Social Flow asks the operator to approve each draft upload. Review the video in TikTok before choosing its caption, cover, and visibility. TikTok controls its API availability, account eligibility, and posting rules.

Testing and support

The integration is in testing. Availability can change as TikTok reviews the application and updates its service. You can disconnect the integration in TikTok settings. For help, contact the maintainers using the link below, without posting secrets or personal information.
