Privacy policy

This policy covers the public Pundago Studio documentation site and the Pundago Studio TikTok integration.

Documentation site

GitHub Pages hosts this static site. It has no login, analytics scripts, or upload form. GitHub may process visitor information to serve and protect the site under its privacy statement.

TikTok connection

Login Kit sends you to TikTok to sign in and approve access. Pundago Studio receives an authorization code, exchanges it for access and refresh tokens, and reads account information such as your TikTok username and an app-specific account ID to verify the connection.

Video drafts

When the operator approves an upload, Social Flow sends the selected video and upload information to TikTok. TikTok processes the draft under its privacy policy.

Storage and removal

The desktop integration stores OAuth credentials and connection details on the operator’s computer. The public site does not receive these credentials or videos. You can revoke access in TikTok settings and ask the maintainers to remove local connection data. Please avoid including credentials, account details, or personal information in a public issue.
